Privacy Policy

Content Poster is a private tool operated by an individual developer to schedule and publish short videos to the operator's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

When the operator connects a TikTok account, the tool receives that account's basic profile information (username and open ID) and permission to upload or publish videos to that same account. It does not access other people's data, messages, followers or private content.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, encrypted with the operating system's built-in protection. They are not sent to any third party, sold, or shared. The operator can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings, which also stops the tool from posting.

Content

Videos posted by the tool are created by the operator. The tool does not collect or store viewers' personal information.
